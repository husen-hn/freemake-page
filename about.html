About Freemake

A simple and free application for converting video and audio.

Freemake

Version 0.0.1 — Beta

Freemake is a flexible media conversion app that gives you control over how your video and audio files are converted. Choose formats, containers, resolutions, bitrate, codecs, and other settings to create the output you need.
